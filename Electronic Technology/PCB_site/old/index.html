Overview

Use this section for the project brief. Explain the goal, expected software, and the final artifact students or visitors must produce.

Concepts

Introduce printed circuit boards, schematic capture, footprint assignment, PCB layout, and design-rule checking.

Why this format works

Pair concise explanations with supporting visuals so the page stays readable and visually engaging.

Requirements

  • Produce a valid schematic for the proposed circuit.
  • Generate a PCB layout that passes your chosen design checks.
  • Use consistent symbols, footprints, and naming conventions.
  • Document the workflow with screenshots or short notes.

Instructions

  1. Create the schematic and annotate all components.
  2. Assign footprints and run your electrical rules check.
  3. Switch to PCB layout and place the components logically.
  4. Route tracks, review clearances, and verify the layout.
  5. Export the required manufacturing or submission files.
