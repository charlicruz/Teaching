🎯 Objetivo del repositorio

  • Centralizar apuntes, ejercicios y materiales de electrónica analógica y digital básica.

💡 Electronics Fundamentals University of Alcalá (UAH)

Welcome to the repository of resources developed for the Digital Electronics component of the Electronics Fundamentals course, taught in several engineering degrees at the University of Alcalá (UAH).

📚 Official course page

Open official course page (UAH)

Note: the official page is available in Spanish.

🎯 Repository goals

  • Centralize notes, exercises, and learning materials.
  • Publish circuit exercises, lab practices, and simulations.
  • Provide explanatory videos, diagrams, and useful tools.
  • Continuously evolve with new resources and examples.
