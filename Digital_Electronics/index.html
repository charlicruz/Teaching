🔌 Digital Electronics University of Alcalá (UAH)

Welcome to this general repository of materials, exercises, and multimedia resources related to the Digital Electronics course, taught across several engineering Bachelor’s programmes at the University of Alcalá (UAH).

📚 Official course page

Syllabus and institutional information for the course:

Open official page (UAH)

Direct link: https://www.uah.es/es/estudios/estudios-oficiales/grados/asignatura/Electronica-Digital-350007/

🎯 Repository purpose

This space aims to:

  • Organize and share original content created for the course.
  • Collect practical exercises, design projects, and learning resources.
  • List and track explanatory videos developed to support learning.
  • Link to public repositories with exercises, simulations, and documentation.

Note: this repository will be progressively updated with new material.

📂 Repository structure

/Digital_Electronics/
  /videos/             # Recorded lessons and tutorials
  /exercises/          # Proposed and solved exercises
  /projects/           # Digital design projects
  /simulations/        # VHDL / Verilog simulation
  /fpga/               # FPGA implementations
