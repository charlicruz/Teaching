# Electronics Design with VHDL: Questions

This repository contains a comprehensive set of VHDL-focused practice questions from the resource **"Electronics Design with VHDL: Questions"** by **Dr. Carlos Cruz de la Torre**, University of Alcalá. The material is designed to help students and advanced learners strengthen their understanding of digital systems design using VHDL.

📄 **DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.13854051](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13854051)

## 📘 Overview

The questions cover key aspects of VHDL and digital design, providing a hands-on approach to:

- **Data Type Conversion**  
  Exercises on converting `std_logic_vector` signals to integers and vice versa.

- **Logical and Relational Operators**  
  Validating logical operations and comparisons between signal values.

- **Array, Record, and Signal Manipulation**  
  Tasks involving bit-level operations on vectors, arrays, and record types.

- **VHDL Code Simulation**  
  Challenges focusing on signal vs. variable behavior, arithmetic operations, and process simulation.

- **Architectural Styles**  
  Practice implementing multiplexers, adders, and flip-flops in:
  - Algorithmic style
  - Dataflow style
  - Structural style

- **Design and Simulation Tasks**  
  Exercises for designing and testing:
  - Multiplexers
  - Adders and full adders
  - Counters
  - Logic gates
  - D flip-flops

- **Advanced Topics**  
  Covers VHDL attributes, type compatibility, and usage of libraries for signed and unsigned arithmetic.

## 🎓 Target Audience

This material is ideal for:
- Undergraduate and graduate students studying digital design or computer engineering
- Learners preparing for practical VHDL exams
- Anyone interested in mastering VHDL and digital circuit design
